whenToConsult:
- You are a new patient.
- Your last exam was more than a year ago.
- You feel that "something is wrong," but you don't know what.
- You want a second opinion before a major treatment.
steps:
- We listen to your needs and your goals.
- We review your health and your medications.
- We examine your teeth, your gums, the tissues of your mouth and your jaw.
- We take X-rays only if they are useful.
- We bring it all together and give you a written report.
- We present your options, at the office, and you decide.
goodToKnow: The exam describes your mouth at one point in time. A further exam or the opinion of a dental specialist may be recommended.
faq:
- q: Do I need X-rays at every visit?

- q: Do I have to start treatment after the exam?
  a: No. You leave with the information; the decision is yours.
