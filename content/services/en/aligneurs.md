intro:
- Aligners are clear trays that you change regularly to move your teeth little by little. The treatment is planned on a computer from a digital impression. Before moving any teeth, we make sure your gums and teeth are healthy.
whenToConsult:
- Overlapping teeth or gaps between the teeth.
- Teeth that have shifted over the years.
- Getting ready for a crown, a bridge or veneers, to place the teeth better.
steps:
- Comprehensive exam, photos and digital impression.
- Planning of the tooth movement; you see the planned result before you decide.
- Wearing the aligners about 22 hours a day, with follow-up visits.
- End of treatment and a retainer to keep the teeth in place.
goodToKnow: Aligners are not right for every case. Complex cases are referred to an orthodontist. The result depends a lot on wearing the trays regularly.
