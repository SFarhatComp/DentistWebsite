title: Implant-supported dentures
shortDescription: Stabilising a denture or replacing teeth on implants.
surtitre: '[ Crowns, bridges and dentures ]'
intro: >-
  Stabilising a denture or replacing teeth with the support of implants. The
  surgical placement is done by a colleague; the prosthesis is designed and made
  in our workshop.
whenToConsult:
  - 'Your lower denture does not hold, despite adjustments.'
  - You want to stabilise an existing appliance.
  - >-
    Several teeth are missing and you are looking for a solution that does not
    move.
bonASavoir: >-
  An implant needs enough bone and healthy gums. The examination and imaging
  tell us whether that is the case before any commitment.
treatmentSteps:
  - 'Examination, imaging and assessment of bone volume.'
  - Planning of the implant positions and of the prosthesis they will carry.
  - 'Surgical placement by a colleague, then a healing period.'
  - Impressions and fabrication of the prosthesis in the workshop.
  - 'Fitting, adjustment of the bite, then upkeep and regular checks.'
faq:
  - question: Who performs the surgery?
    answer: >-
      A surgical colleague. We handle the planning, the prosthesis and the
      follow-up, in coordination with them.
  - question: How long does the treatment take?
    answer: >-
      Several months, the time needed for the bone to heal. The schedule is
      given to you in writing at the outset.
