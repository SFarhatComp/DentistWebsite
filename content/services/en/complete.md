whenToConsult:
- Your denture is more than a few years old and no longer stays in place well.
- You have sore spots, or trouble chewing or speaking.
- You have a few teeth left that cannot be saved.
- You want to stabilize your denture (see Implant dentures).
steps:
- Exam of your mouth, your gums and your current denture.
- Precise impressions of your gums.
- Recording the height and position of your jaw.
- "Try-in of wax or 3D-printed teeth: you approve the look before the final denture is made."
- Delivery and adjustments.
goodToKnow: The bone under a denture changes over the years. A reline or a replacement will be needed one day. A yearly exam also lets us check the tissues of your mouth.
