intro:
- Implants can hold a denture so it moves less, or support fixed teeth. The implants are placed by a colleague we refer you to. We plan the result, make the denture in our dental laboratory and provide the follow-up.
whenToConsult:
- Your lower denture moves when you eat or talk.
- You want fixed teeth instead of a removable denture.
- You already have implants and your denture is worn.
steps:
- Comprehensive exam and planning of the final result.
- Referral to a colleague for implant placement.
- A few months of healing, depending on your case.
- Making and placing the denture at the studio.
- A baseline X-ray, then regular follow-up of the implants and gums.
goodToKnow: An implant can get infected, just like a tooth. Cleaning around the implants and follow-up visits are essential.
faq:
- q: How many implants are needed?
  a: It depends on the jaw, the bone and the type of denture. We explain the options at the exam.
- q: Can my current denture be adapted?
  a: Sometimes. We assess it with you.
