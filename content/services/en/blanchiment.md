  Whitening acts only on natural enamel. Crowns, veneers and fillings keep their
  shade: if they are visible, that has to be taken into account in the plan.
treatmentSteps:
  - >-
    A prior examination: decay, gums and sensitivity are checked before anything
    is undertaken.
  - Recording of the starting shade and photographs.
  - Digital impression and fabrication of the trays in the workshop.
  - >-
    Explanation of the product, the duration and the frequency, then supervised
    application.
  - 'A check of the result and of sensitivity, and recording of the final shade.'

      It varies from person to person, with diet and habits. Occasional upkeep
