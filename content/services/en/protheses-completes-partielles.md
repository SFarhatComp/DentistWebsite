  - 'Examination of the remaining teeth, the gums and the supporting bone.'

  - >-

  - 'Fabrication in the workshop, then delivery and adjustment.'
  - 'Close follow-up adjustments, then upkeep at the regular visits.'

    answer: >-
      Adaptation is gradual. You start with softer textures, and the appliance
