slug: extraction
title: Extraction
shortDescription: 'When a tooth cannot be kept, wisdom teeth included.'
surtitre: '[ Care of teeth and gums ]'
intro: >-
  When a tooth cannot be kept, it is removed — and the question of replacing it
  is discussed before the extraction, not after.
whenToConsult:
  - Decay has destroyed too much of the tooth.
  - The tooth is fractured below the gum.
  - Advanced gum disease has cost the bone support.
  - A wisdom tooth causes repeated infections or has no room to come in.
bonASavoir: >-
  Removing a tooth leaves a space, and the neighbouring teeth can shift.
  Replacement is discussed before the extraction.
treatmentSteps:
  - >-
    Examination and radiograph to assess the root, the bone and the nearby
    structures.
  - 'Review of your medical history and medications: some call for precautions.'
  - 'Local anaesthetic, then extraction.'
  - 'Written instructions, control of bleeding and management of discomfort.'
  - 'A check of the healing, then a discussion of replacement if indicated.'
faq:
  - question: Is it painful?
    answer: >-
      The local anaesthetic makes the procedure painless. The discomfort comes
      mostly in the following days; we explain how to manage it.
  - question: Does the tooth always have to be replaced?
    answer: >-
      Not always. It depends on the tooth, on your bite and on your goals. We
      present the options, including doing nothing, and what that entails.
