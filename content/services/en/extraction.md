- When a tooth cannot be saved, we remove it under local anesthesia and plan what comes next. Before suggesting an extraction, we explain why the tooth cannot be saved and what the options are to replace it, or not.
whenToConsult:
- A tooth that is badly decayed, broken below the gum or very loose.
- A wisdom tooth that hurts, gets infected or damages the tooth next to it.
- An extraction requested before orthodontic treatment.
steps:
- Exam and X-ray.
- Review of your health and your medications.
- Local anesthetic and extraction.
- Written instructions for the days that follow.
- A follow-up call in the days after, as after any surgical treatment.
goodToKnow: Tell us if you take blood thinners or a bone medication (bisphosphonates or another), even if you took it a long time ago. Some complex wisdom teeth are referred to an oral and maxillofacial surgeon.
