title: Occlusal splint
shortDescription: Protecting the teeth if you grind or clench. Made in the workshop.
surtitre: '[ Prevention and follow-up ]'
intro: >-
  A splint custom-made in the workshop, to protect the teeth when you clench or
  grind, by day or by night.
whenToConsult:
  - You wake with a tense or sore jaw.
  - People around you hear you grinding at night.
  - Wear is visible on the edges of your teeth.
  - Your restorations fracture or come loose repeatedly.
bonASavoir: >-
  The splint protects the teeth from wear; it does not stop the clenching, which
  has other causes. We discuss those at the examination.
treatmentSteps:
  - 'Examination of the wear, the bite and the jaw joints.'
  - Digital impression and recording of the bite.
  - Fabrication of the splint in the workshop.
  - 'Try-in and adjustment of the contacts, so the bite is balanced.'
  - 'A check after a few weeks, then at the regular follow-up.'
faq:
  - question: Do I have to wear it every night?
    answer: >-
      That is what is generally recommended for it to protect. We adjust
      according to what we see in the wear and in your comfort.
  - question: Will it fix my headaches?
    answer: >-
      We cannot promise that: headaches have several possible causes. The splint
      is aimed first at protecting the teeth.
