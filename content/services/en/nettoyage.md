whenToConsult:
- Your gums bleed when you brush or floss.
- You have bad breath that does not go away.
- Your teeth look longer or feel loose.
- Your last cleaning was more than a year ago.
steps:
- We measure the health of your gums, tooth by tooth.
- We remove the tartar and plaque, then polish.
- If your gums are diseased, we do root planing under local anesthetic, without surgery, over several visits, followed by a re-evaluation.
- "Together, we set your next maintenance visit: every 3, 4, 6, 9 or 12 months, depending on the health of your gums."
goodToKnow: "The time between two cleanings is not the same for everyone; it depends on your gums, your cavities and your health. Gum disease and diabetes are linked: each one can make the other harder to control. Tell us about your health. Surgical gum treatments (grafts, flap surgery) are referred to a periodontist."
