- "Dr Bader Ramdani, general dentist, does not place implants. We do what comes before and after: the assessment, the planning, the crown or denture, and the long-term follow-up. Implant placement is entrusted to a colleague, and we remain your point of contact."
whenToConsult:
- You are missing one or more teeth.
- A tooth needs to be extracted and you are thinking about an implant.
- You have an implant that has not been checked in a long time.
steps:
- "Comprehensive exam: first, we make sure your mouth is healthy (gums, cavities)."
- We present all the options, implant or not, with their benefits and their limits.
- If an implant is chosen, we refer you to a colleague for placement. A 3D X-ray (CT scan), if needed, is done at that colleague's office or at a radiology centre; the studio does not have one.
- After healing, we make and place the crown or denture.
- We check the implant at every visit.
goodToKnow: An implant is not always the only solution. A bridge or a denture may also be suitable. Smoking and poorly controlled diabetes increase the risks; talk to us about it.
faq:
- q: Why not do everything in one place?
  a: Placement is surgery, and we refer it to a colleague who performs it. The plan stays coordinated by the studio.
- q: How long does the full treatment take?
  a: Several months, mainly because the bone needs time to heal. We give you a schedule at the exam.
