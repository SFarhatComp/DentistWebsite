intro:
- A partial denture replaces a few missing teeth. It clips onto the teeth you still have and comes out for cleaning. Most of the work is done in our dental laboratory, which makes try-ins and adjustments easier.
whenToConsult:
- You are missing teeth and have trouble chewing.
- Your current denture moves, hurts or is broken.
- You are going to lose teeth and want to know how to replace them.
steps:
- "Comprehensive exam: the teeth you still have must be healthy to support the denture."
- Impressions and a record of your bite.
- Try-ins in your mouth to check the shape, the shade and the comfort.
- Delivery of the denture and care instructions.
- Adjustment visits in the weeks that follow.
options: an acrylic denture or a metal-framework denture, which is thinner and more stable. The metal framework is cast by a partner dental laboratory; the rest is made here.
goodToKnow: A new denture takes some getting used to. Small sore spots are common at first; adjustments are part of the service.
faq:
- q: Is it covered?
  a: The Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP) and the RAMQ cover some dentures depending on your situation, sometimes with prior authorization. We check with you before we start.
- q: How long does it take?
  a: Several appointments over a few weeks. We give you the schedule at the exam.
