title: Targeted consultation
shortDescription: >-
  30 minutes for one specific question: a denture, aesthetics or a second
  opinion.
surtitre: '[ Examinations and consultations ]'
intro: >-
  A 30-minute appointment for one specific question: a denture, an aesthetic
  concern, a second opinion on a plan proposed elsewhere, or a discomfort whose
  urgency you want to know.
whenToConsult:
  - You have a specific question about an existing denture or restoration.
  - You would like a second opinion on a treatment plan proposed elsewhere.
  - >-
    You are hesitating over an aesthetic treatment and want to understand the
    options.
  - >-
    You have a localised discomfort and want to know whether it needs prompt
    attention.
bonASavoir: >-
  A targeted consultation answers one question; it does not give a picture of
  the whole mouth. For that, a complete examination is needed.
treatmentSteps:
  - A conversation about the specific reason for your visit.
  - An examination limited to the area or the question concerned.
  - A radiograph only if it answers that question.
  - >-
    An explanation of the possible options, what follows from each, and what
    happens if nothing is done.
faq:
  - question: Does this replace a complete examination?
    answer: >-
      No. A targeted consultation deals with one specific subject. If you want
      an overall picture and a care plan, book the complete examination.
  - question: Can I come only for a second opinion?
    answer: >-
      Yes. Bring the proposed plan and your recent radiographs if you have them:
      that often spares you new ones.
