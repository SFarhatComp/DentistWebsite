slug: examen-parodontal
title: Periodontal examination
shortDescription: A detailed assessment of the gums and of the bone that supports the teeth.
surtitre: '[ Examinations and consultations ]'
intro: >-
  A detailed assessment of the gums and of the bone that supports the teeth. We
  measure, we record and we explain: gum disease is often painless and cannot be
  guessed at.
whenToConsult:
  - Your gums bleed when you brush or floss.
  - You notice persistent bad breath.
  - A tooth is loose or has shifted.
  - You have already been told about gum disease.
  - >-
    You smoke, or you live with diabetes: two factors that weigh on the
    periodontium.
bonASavoir: >-
  Gum disease often progresses without pain. That is why we measure it rather
  than estimate it.
treatmentSteps:
  - 'Probing of the depths around each tooth, and recording of bleeding.'
  - 'Recording of recession, mobility and areas of inflammation.'
  - 'Radiographs to measure the bone level, and photographs if needed.'
  - >-
    Explanation of the diagnosis, the prognosis for each tooth and the proposed
    plan.
faq:
  - question: Is it painful?
    answer: >-
      Probing is uncomfortable where the gum is inflamed, rarely elsewhere. Tell
      us: we can slow down or pause.
  - question: How long does the examination take?
    answer: >-
      About 45 to 60 minutes, depending on the number of teeth and the extent of
      the inflammation.
