intro:
- Good habits start early. We see children for short, reassuring visits, where we explain before we do anything. For children under 10, the RAMQ covers several services, including the exam.
whenToConsult:
- As soon as the first teeth come in, for a gentle first visit.
- A white or brown spot on a tooth.
- A broken tooth after a fall.
steps:
- Your child gets to know the room and the chair.
- An exam suited to their age, on your lap for the little ones.
- "Advice for the parent: brushing, snacks, bottle, toothpaste."
- Preventive care or treatment as needed.
goodToKnow: "From the first tooth, brush twice a day with a fluoride toothpaste. Under 3: the size of a grain of rice. From 3 to 6: the size of a small pea. An adult puts the toothpaste on the brush and helps with brushing; the child spits without rinsing."
faq:
- q: My child is afraid. What can I do?
  a: Talk about it in a simple, positive way. We take our time, and we can do a first visit just to get familiar.
- q: Are baby teeth important?
  a: Yes. They are used for eating and speaking, and they hold the space for the permanent teeth.
