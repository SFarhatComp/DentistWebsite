- "As we age, the mouth changes: receding gums, exposed roots, dry mouth caused by some medications, dentures that need to be remade. We take the time to explain, to write down the instructions and to answer your loved ones' questions, if you wish."
whenToConsult:
- Dry mouth, a burning feeling or sores that do not heal.
- Teeth that break, or cavities near the gums.
- A denture that hurts or no longer stays in.
- You have not seen a dentist in a long time.
steps:
- A full review of your medications and your health.
- An exam of your mouth, including the soft tissues.
- A simple written plan that you can show your loved ones.
- Follow-up visits based on your risk.
goodToKnow: "Many medications dry out the mouth, which increases the risk of cavities. Always bring your up-to-date list. The Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP) is accepted. The studio is on the second floor, up a flight of stairs, with no wheelchair access: call us if stairs are difficult for you."
faq:
- q: Can a loved one come with me?
  a: Yes, with your consent, during the exam and the explanation of the plan.
- q: I wear a complete denture. Do I still need to come in?
  a: "Yes: a yearly exam checks your gums, your tongue and the tissues of your mouth, as well as how your denture fits."
