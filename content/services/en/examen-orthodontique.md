title: Orthodontic examination
shortDescription: An assessment of tooth position and of the bite.
surtitre: '[ Examinations and consultations ]'
intro: >-
  An assessment of tooth position and of the bite — the way the upper and lower
  teeth meet.
whenToConsult:
  - Your teeth overlap or have shifted over time.
  - 'Your bite has changed, or you chew on one side only.'
  - You are considering aligners and want to know whether your case suits them.
  - >-
    In children: permanent teeth are coming in crooked, or baby teeth are
    falling out unevenly.
bonASavoir: >-
  Some cases go beyond what a general dentist treats. When that is so, we refer
  you to an orthodontist — and we say so at the examination, not afterwards.
treatmentSteps:
  - 'Examination of alignment, of the bite and of the space available.'
  - Photographs and a digital impression.
  - A panoramic radiograph if needed.
  - >-
    Explanation of the options and their limits, and referral to a specialist if
    the case calls for it.
faq:
  - question: At what age should a child be seen?
    answer: >-
      A first look around 7 or 8 lets us spot what is easier to correct early.
      That does not mean treatment starts at that age.
  - question: Do I need a referral?
    answer: No. You can book directly.
