- A crown covers a weak tooth to protect it. A bridge replaces a missing tooth by resting on the teeth next to it. At Studio De Facto, these pieces are designed digitally and made in our dental laboratory, in zirconia or ceramic.
whenToConsult:
- A tooth is cracked or has lost a large part of its structure.
- A tooth has had a root canal treatment (especially at the back of the mouth).
- An old crown has come loose, is broken or does not fit well.
- You are missing a tooth and want a fixed solution.
steps:
- "We check that the tooth and the gum are healthy: we don't build on sand."
- We prepare the tooth and take a digital impression, with no impression paste.
- You leave with a temporary crown.
- Our dental laboratory makes the crown; the dentist can fine-tune the shade with the lab team.
- We place the crown and check your bite.
goodToKnow: A crown needs the same care as a natural tooth. Decay can still form at the gum line and on the root underneath.
