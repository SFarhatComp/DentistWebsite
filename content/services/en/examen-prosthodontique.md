intro: >-
  An assessment of existing crowns, bridges and dentures, or of teeth to be
  replaced, before deciding what comes next.
whenToConsult:
  - 'A denture no longer holds, hurts, or has fractured.'
  - A crown or bridge has come loose.
  - One or more teeth are missing and you want to know the options.
  - >-
    You are considering implants and want to know whether your mouth is suited
    to them.
bonASavoir: >-
  Replacing a tooth is not always the first thing to do. We look first at the
  state of the neighbouring teeth, the gums and the bone that will carry the
  solution.
treatmentSteps:
  - 'Examination of the remaining teeth, the gums and the bone support.'
  - Assessment of the dentures or restorations already in the mouth.
  - Study impressions and photographs; radiographs as indicated.
  - >-
    Presentation of the possible options, with their advantages, their limits
    and their upkeep.
faq:
  - question: Should I bring my current denture?
    answer: >-
      Yes, even if you no longer wear it: it tells us what worked and what did
      not hold.
