  A guard custom-made in the workshop, according to your sport, your trade or
  your activity.
whenToConsult:
  - 'You play a contact sport, or one with a risk of falling.'
  - Your work exposes your face to impacts.
  - You wear a store-bought guard that fits poorly or makes breathing difficult.
bonASavoir: >-
  A custom guard follows the shape of your teeth, which makes it more stable and
  less intrusive than a boil-and-bite model.
treatmentSteps:
  - Examination of the teeth and the bite.
  - Digital impression of the arches.
  - 'Fabrication in the workshop, in the thickness suited to your activity.'
  - Try-in and adjustment.

  - question: Does it need replacing?
    answer: >-
