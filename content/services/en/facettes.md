intro:
- A veneer is a thin layer of ceramic bonded to the front of a tooth. It changes the shape, the length or the shade. The result is planned digitally and tried in your mouth before it is made in our dental laboratory.
whenToConsult:
- Front teeth that are worn, chipped or uneven in shape.
- Stains that whitening does not remove.
- Small gaps between the front teeth.
steps:
- "Comprehensive exam: healthy gums and a stable bite come first."
- Photos, digital impression and a smile mock-up.
- A try-in of the planned shape in your mouth, before any work on the teeth.
- Minimal preparation of the teeth, if needed.
- Fabrication in the dental laboratory, then bonding.
goodToKnow: Once the teeth are prepared, the treatment cannot be reversed. If you clench or grind your teeth, a night guard will protect the veneers. Sometimes, aligner treatment before veneers means less work on the teeth.
