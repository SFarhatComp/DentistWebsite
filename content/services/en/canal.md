intro:
- Root canal treatment treats the inside of a tooth that is infected or badly inflamed. It often lets you keep a tooth that would otherwise have to be extracted. We clean the canals of the root, then seal them.
whenToConsult:
- Pain that lingers after something hot or cold.
- Pain that wakes you up at night.
- A swollen gum or a small pimple near a tooth.
- A tooth that is turning darker.
steps:
- Exam, tests and X-ray to confirm the diagnosis.
- Freezing the tooth and placing a small dental dam to isolate it.
- Cleaning and disinfecting the canals.
- Sealing the canals and filling the tooth.
- Often, a crown then protects the tooth.
goodToKnow: A treated tooth becomes more fragile. For back teeth, a crown is generally recommended. A follow-up X-ray is taken at least one year after the treatment. Complex cases are referred to an endodontist. We check in with you in the days after the treatment.
faq:
- q: Does it hurt?
  a: The treatment is done under local anesthetic. Some sensitivity for a few days afterwards is possible.
- q: Do I need antibiotics?
  a: "Not in most cases: treating the tooth is what solves the problem."
