  Applying fluoride at home to strengthen enamel, if your decay risk warrants

intro: >-
  A tray custom-made in the workshop, to apply fluoride at home when the risk of
  decay warrants it.
whenToConsult:
  - You have had several cavities in a short time.
  - 'Your mouth is dry, from a medication or a medical condition.'
  - 'You have many restorations, or an orthodontic appliance.'
  - You have had radiotherapy to the head or neck.
bonASavoir: >-
  The fluoride tray is added to brushing and interdental cleaning: it does not
  replace them.
treatmentSteps:
  - Assessment of your decay risk at the examination.
  - Digital impression of the arches.
  - Fabrication of the tray in the workshop.
  - >-
    Try-in, and an explanation of the product, the duration and the frequency of
    application.
  - 'Reassessment of the risk at follow-up, to adjust or stop.'
faq:
  - question: How long does it need to be worn?
    answer: >-
      A few minutes per application, depending on the product prescribed. How
      long the programme lasts depends on how your risk evolves.
