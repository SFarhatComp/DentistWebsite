## Initial Problem

The patient wanted to correct mild crowding of the lower incisors and the rotation of an upper incisor, while preserving the health of the periodontal tissues.

## Approach

A digital scan allowed us to model an eight-month aligner treatment plan. Wear was monitored at every visit and small adjustments were made along the way.

## Result

The teeth were gradually aligned and a fixed lingual retainer was placed to stabilize the result. A discreet esthetic harmonization was then proposed to finalize the smile.
