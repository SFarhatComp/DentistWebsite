## Initial Problem

The patient presented with marked wear of the incisal edges of the upper incisors, the result of years of abrasion and successive minor fractures. The overall shade was also considered unsatisfactory.

## Approach

After a photographic analysis and an approved mock-up, the treatment plan favored a layered direct composite restoration, without invasive preparation. The shade was adjusted progressively and the final polishing allowed the restorations to blend seamlessly into the natural dentition.

## Result

The incisal edges were rebuilt, tooth length harmonized, and the shade unified. The procedure preserved the existing structure and remains reversible if needed.
