## Initial Problem

The molar had been devitalized and temporarily restored after a cuspal fracture. The remaining structure required full coverage to prevent a catastrophic fracture.

## Approach

A digital impression was taken after a conservative preparation. The design and milling of the crown were carried out at the integrated laboratory, which allowed precise adjustment of shade and occlusion.

## Result

The crown integrates naturally, the occlusal contacts are balanced, and the patient resumed comfortable chewing as soon as it was cemented.
