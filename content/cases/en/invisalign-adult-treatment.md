## Challenge

A 35-year-old professional came to us wanting to straighten their teeth but was hesitant about traditional braces. Their career involved frequent client meetings and presentations, making discretion a priority. They had moderate crowding in both arches and a slight overbite.

Key concerns:
- Moderate crowding affecting smile aesthetics
- Slight overbite causing functional issues
- Need for discreet treatment option
- Busy schedule requiring minimal appointments

## Approach

Invisalign was the perfect solution for this patient's needs and lifestyle:

- **Digital scanning** for precise 3D modeling of teeth
- **ClinCheck treatment simulation** showing predicted results
- **Custom aligner series** of 28 sets over 14 months
- **Minimal refinement aligners** for final perfection
- **Retainer protocol** to maintain results

We scheduled appointments around the patient's work commitments and provided detailed guidance for aligner care during travel.

## Result

After 14 months of treatment, the patient achieved beautifully aligned teeth without anyone at work knowing they were undergoing orthodontic treatment. The transformation has given them renewed confidence in both personal and professional settings.

Final results:
- Perfectly aligned teeth in both arches
- Corrected overbite for improved function
- Zero disruption to professional life
- Maintained results with nighttime retainer wear
