## Challenge

This patient presented with severely compromised dentition due to advanced periodontal disease. Most remaining teeth had poor prognosis and were causing significant discomfort. The patient had been avoiding dental care for years and was now facing the need for comprehensive reconstruction.

Primary challenges included:
- Advanced bone loss in multiple areas
- Remaining teeth with mobility and infection
- Significant functional impairment affecting diet
- Psychological impact of deteriorating oral health

## Approach

We developed a phased treatment approach to address all concerns:

- **Comprehensive 3D imaging** and treatment planning using CBCT scans
- **Strategic extraction** of hopeless teeth with immediate bone grafting
- **Implant placement** using guided surgery for optimal positioning
- **Immediate provisional restoration** to maintain function and aesthetics
- **Final custom prosthetic** designed for optimal fit and natural appearance

Throughout the process, we ensured the patient was never without teeth, providing immediate provisional restorations at each stage.

## Result

After 8 months of treatment, the patient has a fully functional, beautiful smile. The implant-supported restoration provides stability comparable to natural teeth, allowing them to eat all their favorite foods again.

Achieved outcomes:
- Full restoration of chewing function
- Natural-appearing smile aesthetics
- Stable, long-term solution
- Dramatic improvement in quality of life
