whenToConsult:
  - If your gums bleed when brushing
  - If you notice recession or mobility
  - If you have persistent bad breath
  - For periodontal follow-up after treatment
treatmentSteps:
  - Complete periodontal probing (measurements at 6 sites per tooth)
  - Radiographs to evaluate bone support
  - Scaling and root planing
  - Specific hygiene advice
  - Scheduled follow-ups based on severity
options:
  - Deep scaling (by quadrant or by sextant)
  - Root planing
  - Treatment of recessions
  - Periodontal maintenance
limits: Advanced periodontal disease can lead to irreversible loss of support. Treatment aims to stabilize and preserve, not to rebuild lost bone without specialized surgery.
aftercare: Gum and thermal sensitivity is possible for a few days. Meticulous hygiene is essential for stabilization.
faq:
  - question: Is deep scaling painful?
    answer: Performed under local anesthesia when necessary, the treatment is comfortable.
  - question: Will my gums grow back?
    answer: Not spontaneously. Some cases may benefit from grafts, evaluated on a case-by-case basis.
  - question: How long before I see an improvement?
    answer: Inflammation generally decreases within 4 to 8 weeks after the initial treatment, with adequate hygiene.

The health of your gums determines the longevity of your teeth. Successful periodontal treatment relies on a precise diagnosis, rigorous initial treatment and regular follow-up.

The studio offers a detailed periodontal assessment with complete probing and imaging as needed. The treatment plan is explained clearly, including the patient's responsibilities regarding hygiene.
