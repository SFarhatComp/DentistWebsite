Prevention remains the foundation of lasting oral health. Rather than waiting for problems to appear, we assess risk factors, adjust habits and plan recall visits tailored to each patient.

Every hygiene appointment begins with a periodontal assessment, followed by scaling adapted to the deposits present. At-home hygiene advice is concrete and tailored to your dentition.
