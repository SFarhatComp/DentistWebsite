A well-managed dental emergency prevents more serious complications. The studio offers rapid assessment to relieve pain and plan the definitive treatment under the best conditions.

For a fast response, use the dedicated emergency form. In case of severe symptoms (rapid swelling, difficulty breathing or swallowing, high fever, major trauma), contact emergency services immediately.
