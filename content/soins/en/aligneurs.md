whenToConsult:
  - To correct mild or moderate crowding
  - To close small gaps
  - Before esthetic or prosthetic treatments
  - To stabilize occlusion after treatment
treatmentSteps:
  - Consultation and orthodontic assessment
  - Digital scan and 3D treatment plan
  - Approval of the planned steps
  - Wearing the aligners (22 h/day, changed every 1-2 weeks)
  - Periodic follow-up at the studio
  - Final retention to stabilize the result
options:
  - Simple cases (anterior alignment)
  - Moderate cases (multiple crowding)
  - Orthodontic preparation before veneers or crowns
  - Referral to an orthodontist for complex cases
limits: Aligners do not correct every type of malocclusion. Results depend
  largely on patient cooperation. Long-term stability requires retention.
aftercare: Regular wear of the retainer (ideally for life to preserve
  alignment). Meticulous hygiene throughout treatment.
faq:
  - question: How long does a treatment take?
    answer: From 4 to 18 months depending on the complexity of the case.
  - question: Are the aligners visible?
    answer: Very little. They are clear and discreet in the mouth.
  - question: Do they need to be removed for eating?
    answer: Yes, and also to drink anything other than water, as well as for brushing.

Aligner orthodontics offers a discreet, comfortable approach for correcting mild to moderate crowding. Every treatment begins with a digital scan that lets us visualize the full plan and the expected result.

At the studio, aligners often fit into a broader plan — for example, aligning before restoring to preserve tooth structure and achieve a more natural result.
