A crown or bridge restores not only a tooth but also the balance of the entire smile. The proximity of the integrated laboratory at the studio enables direct communication between clinic and fabrication, which improves precision and personalization.

Each restoration is planned with esthetics, occlusion, and longevity in mind. Materials are selected based on the position of the tooth and the forces it must withstand.
