Cosmetic dentistry is not about imposing a standardized smile, but about harmonizing what exists with what the patient wants. Shape, proportion, color and alignment are assessed together.

At the studio, every cosmetic treatment begins with a complete analysis, a discussion of realistic goals and, when relevant, a mock-up that lets you visualize the result before any irreversible intervention.
