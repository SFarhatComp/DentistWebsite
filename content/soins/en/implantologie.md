An implant replaces the root of a missing tooth. With proper planning, it allows the restoration of a crown, bridge, or prosthesis in a functional and stable way.

The studio uses digital planning to visualize the ideal position before surgery. This approach reduces surprises and optimizes the esthetics and function of the final result.
